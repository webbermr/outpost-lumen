Starting up…

View: Splat (M or X changes it)

In the headset: left stick walks, right stick turns, trigger or grip grabs and throws. A brings the objects back. X flips the view: splat, splat with the collision wireframe, mesh.

Here: drag to look, W A S D to walk, E and Q to raise and lower your view, click an object to pick it up and click again to toss it. R, M and H do the same as A, X and hide this panel.
